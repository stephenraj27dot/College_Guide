College Guide is a professional Tamil Nadu college discovery and student guidance platform.

The platform must help students discover suitable colleges while helping the client generate, manage, and convert student enquiries.

---

## Primary User

### Student

Students should be able to:

- Explore colleges
- Select education category
- Select location
- Search colleges
- Filter colleges
- View college details
- Explore departments
- Explore courses
- View facilities
- Shortlist colleges
- Compare colleges
- Request guidance
- Contact College Guide through WhatsApp

---

## Primary Business Goal

The most important business workflow is:

Student discovers college
        ↓
Student becomes interested
        ↓
Student requests guidance
        ↓
Lead reaches College Guide
        ↓
Counsellor follows up
        ↓
Student receives guidance
        ↓
Admission conversion

## Important Contact Rule

Students must contact College Guide, not the colleges directly.

All student enquiry and WhatsApp actions must use the client's approved contact channels.

Do not expose direct college contact options unless the client explicitly approves them.
